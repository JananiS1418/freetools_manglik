Introduction to
Mangal (Manglik) Dosha

✦

Manglik or Mangal or Kuja Dosha means affliction of Mars (Mangala) in the birth chart and can be regarded as one of the much-feared afflictions in Vedic astrology. It is one of the important factors considered during the horoscope matching of a bride and a groom during a marriage proposal. Mangal (Kuja) Dosha is caused due to the adverse positioning of the planet Mars (Kuja) in your birth chart and can have serious effects on your marital relationship and harmony.

Effects of Mangal (Manglik) Dosha

✦

Mangal or Manglik Dosha is thus an adverse affliction that could result in serious relationship problems when Mars is placed in particular Houses in a birth chart. However, the position of Mars in each of these Houses can cause different effects. When you have Kuja Dosha in your horoscope, you may face one or more of the following adverse effects:

Become egotistical, dominating and aggressive
Health problems
Face delays in marriage
Financial stress and failure
Problems in marital life
Disputes or separation from spouse

Remedies of
Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha Nivarana)

✦

Following are a few simple remedies that you can follow to lessen the effects of Manglik (Kuja) Dosha:

Worship planet Mars and Navagraha (9 planets) on Tuesdays
Propitiate Lord Hanuman and Lord Muruga on Tuesdays
Observe fasts and chant mantras in favor of planet Mars
Do charity, donate blood on Tuesdays and feed birds
Wear a ring with the gemstone coral in the right-hand ring finger

Placement of Mars
Indicating Mangal (Manglik) Dosha

✦

The following are the placements of Mars from Ascendant and Moon in the birth chart, which indicates Mangal (Kuja) Dosha:

Mars in the 1st house
Mars in the 7th house
Mars in the 2nd house
Mars in the 8th house
Mars in the 4th house
Mars in the 12th house

Note: Vedic astrology has given certain exemptions to the above-mentioned placement of Mars indicating Manglik Dosha.

Frequently Asked Questions

✦

What is Mangal Dosha (Manglik Dosha)?

Mangal Dosha is an astrological condition caused by the placement of Mars in certain houses of the birth chart. It is believed to affect marital harmony and relationships.

Which houses indicate Mangal Dosha?

The 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, and 12th houses in the birth chart are typically considered for indicating Mangal Dosha.

Does Mangal Dosha always cause problems in marriage?

No, not always. The effects depend on the exact placement of Mars and other planetary positions. In many cases, the dosha gets canceled automatically.

Can Mangal Dosha be removed or reduced?

Yes, specific Vedic remedies, poojas, and lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the negative impact of Mangal Dosha.

What are the best remedies for Mangal Dosha?

Some common remedies include Kumbh Vivah, chanting Hanuman Chalisa, fasting on Tuesdays, and wearing specific gemstones like Red Coral.

How can I know if I have Mangal Dosha in my horoscope?

You can use an accurate Mangal Dosha calculator or consult a qualified Vedic astrologer to analyze your birth chart.
